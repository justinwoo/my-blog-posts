# I want to get to the Characterization Part

A long time ago, I used to work in a materials science lab doing fabrication and characterization of some fairly simple materials. I spent a lot of time with creating nanostructures using electrochemical cells, chemically preparing the anodized substrate surfaces, using high vacuum deposition to grow structures on these surfaces, and then finally looking at the result of all of this work, which took a lot of planning, hours of execution, and then more hours of measurements and observations.

Readers familiar with materials science will recognize this measurement and observation step as "characterization". See <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Characterization_(materials_science)>.

In my case, I spent many hours operating an Atomic Force Microscope (AFM) to measure my surfaces, many hours operating a Scanning Electron Microscope (SEM) to look at my surfaces, and many hours operating high magnification lens to observe interactions of my surfaces with water droplets.

In software, we also spend a lot of time on making systems up from their parts: the code, the tools we use to build the code, the artifacts that we create from these builds, the deployment of these artifacts that work together to make our systems. We try to profile, observe, and otherwise measure and analyze what is going on with our systems.

The tools we use often have some properties we dislike: being incorrect, being slow to use, causing other tools to be slow and otherwise unresponsive, and so many more. Being able to report some of these undesirable properties, collaborate with the maintainers to fix these properties, or just being able to address the properties ourselves by using resources from the authors of these tools and otherwise is nice, but is increasingly not possible.

This is how I ended up giving up on many of the tools I used and consider using. In today's world, I want to help myself and be given help to help myself better, and to have opportunities to return help in kind.

I leave the interpretation and conclusion of this post to the reader.
